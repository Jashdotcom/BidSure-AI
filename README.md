# BidSure AI

## AI-Powered Bid Compliance & Verification Platform

> **Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026 — Problem Statement: 26100**
>
> BidSure AI is an AI-powered procurement intelligence platform designed to assist government procurement officers in analyzing tender documents, verifying bidder compliance, comparing bids, and generating explainable procurement reports.

---

## Project Status

**Work in Progress — SIH Prototype**

BidSure AI is currently under active development. Some modules are implemented and functional, while other components are still being integrated and tested.

The current development focus is on:

- Real tender ingestion
- AI-powered tender requirement extraction
- Local LLM integration using Ollama
- Grounded evidence extraction
- Officer review and verification
- Real bidder document processing
- Government verification integrations
- Compliance evaluation
- Bid comparison and explainable ranking

Features and architecture may change as development continues.

# 📌 SIH Problem Statement

**Smart India Hackathon 2026 — Problem Statement: 26100**

### AI-Powered Integrated Bid Compliance Verification Platform for GeM Procurement

BidSure AI is being developed as a solution for the SIH problem statement focused on **AI-powered bid compliance and verification in government procurement**.

The platform aims to reduce the manual effort involved in:

- Understanding lengthy tender documents
- Identifying mandatory procurement requirements
- Checking bidder-submitted documents
- Verifying statutory and qualification information
- Evaluating bid compliance
- Comparing multiple bidders
- Maintaining procurement traceability
- Generating explainable procurement reports

> **Problem Statement:** SIH 2026 Problem Statement 4
